This week, **Dr. Thain**, **Alan Rodrigues**, and **Barry** are attending the [eScience'26 conference](https://www.escience-conference.org/2026/) in Naples, Italy. The conference is being held at the Villa Doria d’Angri, an architectural masterpiece belonging to the University of Naples "Parthenope," from September 28 - October 2, 2026.

Barry is presenting his work on: **[Fusion and Reshaping of Hidden Task Graphs for High Energy Physics Applications](/assets/paper/pdf/reshaping-escience-2026.pdf)**.

Alan is presenting his work on: **[Hybrid Workflow Composition for Extreme-Scale Data Processing: A Case Study on the HL-LHC](/assets/paper/pdf/hl-lhc-escience-2026.pdf)**.

Dr. Thain is presenting (on behalf of Saiful): **[Declare, Resolve, Reuse: A Portable Data Layer for Notebook Workflows Across HPC Systems](/assets/paper/pdf/floability-escience-2026.pdf)**.
