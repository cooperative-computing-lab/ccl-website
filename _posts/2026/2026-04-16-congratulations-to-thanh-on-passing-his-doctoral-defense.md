This month we are delighted to congratulate **Thanh Son Phung** on successfully defending his Ph.D. dissertation. Thanh has been part of our group since 2020.

</div>

Thanh’s research sits at the intersection of **distributed systems** and **large-scale scientific computing**. He focused on software that helps researchers decompose massive computational problems into many smaller tasks and run them in parallel efficiently—turning runs that might otherwise take weeks or days into hours or minutes. Along the way he collaborated with scientists across several areas, including molecular chemistry, particle physics, and artificial intelligence, to make their computations faster and more practical on modern clusters.
