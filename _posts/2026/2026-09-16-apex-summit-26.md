This week, CCL fourth-year PhD student **Md Saiful Islam** and second-year PhD student **Ryan Hartung** traveled to Chicago for **[APeX Summit 2026](https://parsl-project.org/parslfest/apex2026.html)**, Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute, held at the University of Chicago's Data Science Institute from September 14–15, 2026. Saiful presented his work **Floability Backpacks: Packaging Workflows for Portable HPC Deployment**.
